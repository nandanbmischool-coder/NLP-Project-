How it works

A classic NLP pipeline, from raw text to a probability.

1

Pre-processing

Whitespace normalisation, sentence segmentation and tokenisation.

2

Feature extraction

Word and character n-gram TF-IDF vectors capture vocabulary and phrasing.

3

Stylometry

Lexical diversity, sentence-length variation, punctuation and word length.

4

Classification

Logistic regression outputs P(AI), with a middle band reported as uncertain.

Limitations

No AI-text detector is perfectly reliable.

  • Output is a probability, not proof, and should not be sole evidence of misconduct.
  • Inputs under 30 words are rejected because short texts are unreliable.
  • Formal or non-native-English human writing can produce false positives.
  • Paraphrased or heavily edited AI text can produce false negatives.
  • The model learned from ChatGPT-era answers; newer models may write differently.
